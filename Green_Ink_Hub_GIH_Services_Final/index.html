01 / ABOUT GIH

Designing with purpose, not just appearance.

Green Ink Hub (GIH) is a multidisciplinary consultancy specialising in architecture, landscape, sustainable solutions, and green building certifications.

We integrate design excellence, environmental responsibility, and technical expertise to create refined, functional, and enduring spaces that are thoughtfully designed for both people and the planet.

02 / SERVICES

What we do.
